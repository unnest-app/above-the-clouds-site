Privacy & terms

Above the Clouds is a small, simulated travel experience. Flights and maps are illustrative, not live flight information.

Your information

This version has no account, analytics, advertising system of its own, or personal information form. Your trip choices stay in memory and reset when you reload. Our hosting provider receives standard connection information when serving this site.

Optional YouTube views

We use the YouTube IFrame Player API. No YouTube player or API is loaded until you choose Explore at your destination. Choosing it allows Google to receive connection and device information and serve the video, including any ads or cookies it uses. We use the privacy-enhanced embed domain; this does not prevent all information sharing with Google.

Using this video feature means you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Read the Google Privacy Policy for Google's practices. You can skip the video and use your postcard, or return to the terminal to stop the player. A new journey starts without loading a video.

External videos are provided by their credited creators on YouTube. We do not download or host copies, own those videos, or guarantee their availability.
